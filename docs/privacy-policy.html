Do or Die: Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026.

Summary

Do or Die does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app records stays on your phone. The app does not request internet access, has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs that send data.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your chosen System, rank, level, XP and daily quest progress.
  • A log of quest results, penalties, lock releases, emergency exits, contested verdicts, settings changes and blocker outages, used for the in-app 30-day report.
  • The apps you chose to lock, your rest period, lock time and app settings.
  • Text you write: your plans for tomorrow (a Personality penalty), the reasons you give for an emergency exit or a contested verdict, and your placement answers.
  • Earlier trials you restarted, kept so you can review them; you can delete them in Settings.

This data is stored in the app's private storage. It is excluded from Android backups and from device-to-device transfer, so it is never copied to your Google account or to another phone. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings. The app can export your log as a file only when you ask it to; sharing that file, or the report image, happens only through Android's share sheet at your request.

Permissions and why they are used

PermissionWhy
Usage access (PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS)To see which app is in front, so a locked app can be covered during a penalty, and to measure screen-time objectives. Read on the device only; never stored outside the app or sent anywhere.
Display over other apps (SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW)To show the Penalty Zone screen over an app you chose to lock.
Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE)Runs the lock watcher only while a lock, a phone-free block or a pending verdict needs it, with a visible notification. It stops otherwise; exact alarms wake it at midnight and just before a lock.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)Quest warnings, penalty notices and reminders.
Exact alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM)So deadline warnings arrive on time. Optional; without it, notices may be delayed.
Run at startup (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED)Restores scheduled notices and the lock watcher after a restart.

The list of installed apps is read on the device only so you can choose which apps to lock. Phone, messages, maps, settings, and banking and payment apps can never be locked.

Age

Do or Die is for adults 18 and over; the app asks you to confirm this before it starts. It does not knowingly collect any data from anyone.
