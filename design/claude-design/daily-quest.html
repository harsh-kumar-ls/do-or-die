NOTES

Every control works. Timers and blocks run on the simulated clock, so use ×30 to watch a plank or block finish.

Self-report. Holds, counts and distance are honour-based. Verify walks with step data and Focus objectives with Usage Access, or the penalty is trivially avoided.

Caps settle at midnight. A "lower is better" cap can't be cleared early, so Focus shows "All objectives complete" with the cap still holding until 23:59. Decide whether exceeding the cap after that still fails the day.

Undo. Counts and distance have a small undo button. Without it, one mis-tap can't be fixed. Undo locks once a row is complete.

Rank D quests here are placeholders until the rank tables exist. Dungeon Floor 2 multiplies targets by 1.5.
